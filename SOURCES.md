## Published top-50 list (data/raw/top50_mcknight.json)

Retrieved 2026-07-05 via web-research agents (all URLs fetched and content-verified):
- 2021 list: McKnight et al., "Identifying the 50 statistically-most-concerning derelict objects in LEO", Acta Astronautica 181 (2021), DOI 10.1016/j.actaastro.2021.01.021 (paywalled) — table taken verbatim from the reproduction in Abdul-Hamid et al., arXiv:2510.07708 (Appendix Table A1), https://arxiv.org/html/2510.07708v1 . No NORAD IDs published; matching to catalog objects by orbit proximity is documented in `shared/match_top50.py` (49/49 matched, median |dalt| 0.8 km, |dinc| 0.019 deg). Known source caveats: rank-27 SL-8 apogee misprinted "9956 km" (left null); Envisat mass 7800 kg here vs 8211 kg elsewhere.
- 2025 interim list: McKnight et al., "An Update of the Top 50 List", 9th European Conference on Space Debris (SDC9-paper4), https://conference.sdo.esoc.esa.int/proceedings/sdc9/paper/4/SDC9-paper4.pdf (not used for gate verdicts; retained for context).
