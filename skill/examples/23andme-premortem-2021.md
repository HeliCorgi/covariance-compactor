# Worked example — a pre-registered "prophecy" on 23andMe (frozen at the June 2021 IPO)

**What this is.** A demonstration of the `falsifying-concepts` skill used as a
*pre-mortem*: we rewind to 23andMe's June 2021 SPAC IPO — when it looked safe —
**pre-register** a falsifiable kill thesis using **only information knowable at
or before June 2021**, issue a verdict, and *then* reveal the actual 2021→2025
outcome to check the call.

**Disclaimer.** Methodology demonstration, not investment advice. Every number is
public information labeled with its basis and source; the 2021 reasoning uses no
post-2021 hindsight. **Honesty caveat (read this):** 23andMe was *selected*
knowing it later failed, so this validates that "the 2021-information reasoning
would have flagged it," **not** a forward hit-rate. A true out-of-sample test
runs the same discipline on a *live* company (see the note at the end).

---

## 1. Pre-registration — frozen with June-2021 information only

**How it looked in June 2021 (the "safe" surface).** 23andMe listed on Nasdaq
("ME") on 2021-06-17 by merging with Richard Branson's VG Acquisition Corp at a
~$3.5B valuation, the #1 consumer-genetics brand with ~11 million genotyped
customers, ~$600M of fresh cash, a celebrity SPAC, and a "genetics + health
subscription + drug discovery" growth story.

**Load-bearing assumption (if false, the company dies):** *the decay of a
one-time-purchase core (a DNA kit you buy once in your life) can be out-run by an
unproven pivot to recurring revenue — the 23andMe+ subscription and a
therapeutics/data-licensing business — before cash runs out.*

**Decision metric(s):** (i) trajectory of core consumer-kit revenue (is the
one-time TAM saturating?); (ii) recurring revenue actually generated by the
pivot; (iii) cash runway vs burn.

**Kill threshold (frozen):** if within **~3–4 years** kit revenue flattens/declines
**and** the subscription/therapeutics pivot has **not** produced material,
durable recurring revenue **and** cash burn continues → existential. **KILL.**

**Null control:** the headline metric "**~11 million cumulative customers**" is
exactly the number a *one-time-purchase* business inflates; it says nothing about
recurring revenue. The evidential metric is repeat/recurring revenue per
customer, which is ≈ 0 for the core product.

**Counterfactual:** for a consumer, a DNA test is a **once-in-a-lifetime**
purchase — the category has no repeat-purchase engine. The drug-discovery pivot
competes with real pharma R&D while carrying no drug revenue for years.

**Falsifiability:** *survive-world* — 23andMe+ and/or the pipeline generate real
recurring revenue that offsets kit decay and reaches profitability. *kill-world* —
kit sales saturate, subscription doesn't scale, therapeutics yields no revenue,
cash burns out.

## 3. Kill-test arithmetic (2021-knowable inputs)

```
Core revenue trend : FY2020 $305.5M -> FY2021 $243.9M  = -20% YoY (already declining, pre-IPO)
Naive cash runway  : ~$600M / ~$184M per yr = ~3.3 years  (burn grows as therapeutics scales -> shorter)

Subscribers needed just to cover the ~$184M/yr loss with 23andMe+ gross revenue:
  at $30/yr : 6.1M paying subs = 51% of the 12M one-time base must convert to recurring
  at $70/yr : 2.6M paying subs = 22% of the 12M one-time base must convert to recurring
```

Converting 22–51% of a **dormant** one-time-buyer base into paying recurring
subscribers — with no demonstrated conversion engine — is a heroic assumption.
The runway (~3.3 years) sits right inside the pre-registered kill window.

**Null control fires:** "12M customers" grows regardless of whether anyone ever
pays again — a vacuous success metric for a recurring-revenue thesis.

## 4. Adversarial review (2021 information only) — the kill SURVIVES

An independent skeptic attacked the kill with the strongest 2021-era bull cases.
Verdict: **the structural kill holds; the bull cases only dent the timing.**

- **Therapeutics blockbuster / out-licensing:** the lead asset was Phase 1 in
  2021 → approval ~2028–2030, revenue in the 2030s (far outside the 3–4-year
  window), and R&D **increases** the ~$184M/yr burn rather than offsetting it;
  a single Phase 1 oncology asset carries ~5–10% approval odds.
- **License the "world's largest genetic database" to many pharma:** the only
  proof point (GSK) is a **fixed-term exclusive** deal that structurally blocks
  selling the same data to others; marginal genome value diminishes past millions
  of samples; no second buyer was visible in 2021.
- **23andMe+ subscription:** at $29 ARPU, even **3M subs (~27% of the base) =
  ~$87M/yr** — only a partial offset to a declining consumer line.
- **Wojcicki control + $600M cash + brand:** buys ~3–5 years of runway; it moves
  the *timing*, not the thesis.

**Any coherent working world?** Only as a **low-probability compound bet** that
needs four independent, unproven things to land together within the runway
(subscription scales *and* ARPU rises; the database becomes a multi-tenant
recurring platform with several buyers; a therapeutics program validates early;
cheap capital stays available). That restates the kill rather than refuting it.

---

## 5. Verdict — as of June 2021

**KILL — high existential risk within ~3–4 years.** A one-time-purchase core that
is *already* declining, an unproven recurring pivot that even under aggressive
assumptions only partly offsets the decline, a no-revenue drug pipeline that
*widens* the burn, and a ~3.3-year cash runway. The load-bearing assumption is
fragile in exactly the way the pre-registered test was built to detect.

## 7. Scorecard — pre-registered thesis vs reality

| Pre-registered kill criterion (June 2021) | What happened | Tripped? |
|---|---|---|
| Kit revenue flattens/declines (one-time TAM saturates) | Revenue peaked then declined into FY24/25 | ✅ |
| Pivot produces no material recurring revenue | 23andMe+ never scaled; **therapeutics wound down Nov 2024** | ✅ |
| Cash burn continues | ~$2.3B accumulated deficit; cash to $79.4M; going concern | ✅ |
| Existential within ~3–4 years | **Chapter 11 in ~3.75 years (Jun 2021 → Mar 2025)** | ✅ |

The prediction, and even its ~3–4-year timing, matched the outcome. The specific
failure the skill flagged — a decaying one-time core plus a pivot that never
generated recurring revenue — is exactly what the November 2024 restructuring
(kill the drug division, no recurring engine) and the March 2025 bankruptcy show.

## 8. Honest limits

- **Retrospective target selection.** 23andMe was chosen knowing it failed, so
  this proves the *2021-information reasoning* was sound, not a validated forward
  hit-rate. The reasoning firewall (2021-only inputs) is real; the selection is
  not out-of-sample.
- **A true forward test** applies the identical discipline to a *live* company —
  a dated, falsifiable kill thesis with a review date — and waits. That is the
  honest way to earn a track record. (The skill is built for exactly that.)
- Single case; public data only; not advice.
